eparagony.pl does not accept a 0.00 zł line (it rejects such a line), so the integration shows the gift at 0.01 zł taken off a paid line in the same rate. The total does not change.

  • Default

    The gift is not on the document. Stock still comes off the warehouse. This is what Shoper and Apilo do.

  • The gift is a 0.00 zł line. When the provider does not accept zero, the integration takes 0.01 zł off a paid line in the same VAT rate.

  • Part of the payment moves to the gift, in proportion to prices, each item in its own rate.

  • The gift does not go on the customer document. OpenLinker records it as a free transfer with its purchase cost.

  • Not available

    A 2026 tax interpretation (KIS) treated such an entry as a free transfer, not a discount.

A set stays one line when every item in it has the same VAT rate. When the rates differ, it is shown as its items, each at its own rate, with the price you charged split in proportion to their prices. If a rate or a price needed for that is missing, the document is held and says why.

The form of the gift is a tax decision. Talk to your accountant or tax adviser.
