Sales documents

How gifts and bundles look on sales documents. The defaults work right away, you do not have to choose anything.

Gifts and bundles

Bundles on documents: one line when all items share a VAT rate, otherwise each item at its own rate.

Gift on the document
  • Default

    The gift is a 0.01 zł line, taken off the most expensive paid item. The total does not change. Default, as BaseLinker does it.

  • Part of the payment moves to the gift, in proportion to prices, each item in its own VAT rate.

  • The gift is a 0.00 line. Offered only where the document provider accepts a zero line.

  • The gift is not on the document. It may be treated as a free transfer, ask your accountant.

This is the OpenLinker-wide default.

The most specific setting wins: the gift itself (gift rule, gift slot in a bundle, manual add), then the sales channel, then this OpenLinker-wide default. Every gift shows where its form came from: "from the rule", "from Allegro settings" or "OpenLinker default".

The form of the gift is a tax decision. Talk to your accountant or tax adviser.

Gifts and bundles

Bundles on documents: one line when all items share a VAT rate, otherwise each item at its own rate.

Gift on the document
  • Default

    The gift is a 0.01 zł line, taken off the most expensive paid item. The total does not change. Default, as BaseLinker does it.

  • Part of the payment moves to the gift, in proportion to prices, each item in its own VAT rate.

  • The gift is not on the document. It may be treated as a free transfer, ask your accountant.
