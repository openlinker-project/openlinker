Set up the pack bench

OpenLinker can do your picking and packing itself, on a screen built for someone standing at a bench with a scanner. Adding the connection changes nothing on its own — until you put OpenLinker in charge below, your shop keeps deciding where orders ship from, exactly as it does now, and that one decision is what stops your shop receiving the orders OpenLinker packs.

Add the OpenLinker OMS

This is the whole form. There is nothing to type in beyond a name.

OL
OpenLinker OMS
No API key, no password, no URL. OpenLinker is already talking to itself.
Adding this changes nothing on its own

Your shop keeps deciding where orders ship from until you put OpenLinker in charge below. Nothing is routed, nothing is held, no order changes path.

Put OpenLinker in charge of where orders ship from?

One of three arrangements on Who decides what. This only affects what happens from now on. Anything already in progress — a parcel someone is packing, a label already bought — keeps its current arrangement until it finishes.

Where does an order ship from?
Wherever the order lands today becomes OpenLinker
OpenLinker will decide this from now on.
Where does the order get created?
Your shop, as it does now becomes OpenLinker only
Orders OpenLinker routes are no longer copied into your shop at all.
Read the second row before you save

It is the one that surprises people. Anything you do in your shop today with these orders — invoices, picking lists, stock counts, the customer’s “my orders” page, a second person packing — stops seeing them. Orders your shop raised itself are untouched.

“Who picks and ships?” is not changed here

That one is granted per parcel, when OpenLinker offers work and your bench accepts it — it follows from the Packer role, not from a setting on this page.

No place to ship from

Fulfilment routing cannot be enabled until at least one active inventory location exists. Create one first — the connection’s routing readiness panel offers a default, or add one under Locations.
