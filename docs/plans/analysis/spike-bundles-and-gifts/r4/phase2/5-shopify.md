## How Shopify models bundles and gifts (summary)

- **A bundle is a parent product whose variants have components** (`ProductVariant.productVariantComponents`, each a `ProductVariantComponent` with `productVariant` + `quantity`). The parent holds no stock of its own; availability is computed from the components.
- **At checkout the parent becomes component lines.** The order holds one `LineItem` per component, each pointing to a `LineItemGroup` (the parent's title, product, variant, SKU, quantity, custom attributes - **no price field**). "Order lines return bundle components, with a reference to the bundle parent." (D-BUND)
- **Money is on the component lines.** A discount on a bundle is computed on the parent, then spread over components by weighted price; tax is computed per component (H-SB).
- **Two build paths**: (1) the free first-party "Shopify Bundles" app (fixed bundles and multipacks, up to 30 components); (2) the Cart Transform Function API for "customized" (mix-and-match) bundles built by apps (`lineExpand`, `linesMerge`, `lineUpdate`).
- **Gifts have no type of their own.** A free item comes from a Buy X Get Y discount ("Free" value) or a manual 100% line discount. The order line keeps its full `originalUnitPriceSet` plus a `DiscountAllocation` pointing at the discount application (title, value, method). There is no `isGift` line field; `LineItem.isGiftCard` is about gift cards, not free gifts.

## Integration normalisation

Shopify is a single channel, not a multi-channel OMS, so it does not normalise other channels' encodings in the sources read. What it does is relevant as (a) the encoding OpenLinker will receive from a Shopify adapter, and (b) design patterns.

- **One canonical order encoding, enforced at the cart.** Whatever the bundle app does (fixed, multipack, mix-and-match, merge or expand), by the time an order exists it is "component lines + group reference". Apps cannot leave a priced parent line on the order. The normalisation happens before the order, inside the Cart Transform function. (D-BUND: "Order lines return bundle components, with a reference to the bundle parent.")
- **Group without money.** `LineItemGroup` has identity and quantity only; all money, discounts and tax live on component lines (D-LIG field list; H-SB weighted allocation). The Shopify adapter in OpenLinker should therefore build our "bundle parent" from the group and sum price from components, never expect a parent price. Caveat: the help page shows "the bundle price" in the nested display - it is derived, not stored on the group (H-ELIG).
- **Conflicting representations between surfaces.** Admin GraphQL puts discounts on components; Liquid (cart) says "discounts are applied to the parent line item" (D-LQ); POS extensions got component-level discount allocations only in 2026-07 (CL-POS). Trust the Admin GraphQL order model for ingestion; it is the order of record.
- **Grouping key exists in three shapes**: GraphQL `LineItem.lineItemGroup`, REST/webhook `sales_line_item_group_id` (since 2025-01, CL-WH), Customer Account API `LineItem.group` (CL-GRP). An adapter must use the one its API version exposes.
- **Capability declared per channel, default off.** For outbound publishing, bundles are excluded from a channel unless the channel turns on "Sell bundles" (D-PUB), and bundle-only variants are auto-hidden from channels that cannot handle them (`requiresComponents`, D-PV). This is a capability flag on the channel, not a per-product decision.
- **Imported/third-party orders are not normalised.** `orderCreate` has no group field and Cart Transform does not run on it (D-OCLI; D-CT "Create Order API: Not supported"). Orders brought into Shopify from elsewhere arrive flat. Marketplace Connect behaviour: Unknown (help blocked).
- **Precedence between several apps is silent.** If two apps' transforms target the same line, "the first lineExpand operation in the list is executed, and all other lineExpand operations for the same cart line are discarded" and expand beats merge (D-CT "Multiple operations").

## UX patterns worth copying

1. **Availability calculator explained in the UI/docs with a worked example** (2 chairs + 1 desk -> 7 bundles), and explicit rule that untracked / oversell-allowed components are ignored (H-ELIG). OpenLinker should show "why is this bundle 7" per component.
2. **List price + allocation per line with a link back to the rule** (`originalUnitPriceSet` + `DiscountAllocation.discountApplication` with title/value/method) - exactly the neutral "list price + reduction" for X-01 and the basis for G-10 documents.
3. **Immutable sales ledger for every placement / edit / refund** with app, staff user, reason, timestamp, and per-line amounts; rounding remainders allocated once and never redistributed (D-SA, Sale interface). A ready-made answer to X-06.
4. **Channel capability opt-in with a safe default** ("Sell bundles" off -> bundles excluded; `requiresComponents` -> hidden where unsupported) (D-PUB, D-PV).
5. **Self-healing definition status**: when a component variant is deleted or renamed, the bundle drops to Draft and the editor shows "Option values were updated on the product and removed from the bundle: <variant/option name>" (H-SB troubleshooting). Better than silently selling a broken bundle.
6. **Combine options with matching names**: one Size selector for all components (H-SB) - a cheap way to do B-09 for apparel sets.

## Anti-patterns (avoid)

1. **The free gift is never added automatically** - the buyer has to put it in the cart, and if it sells out the discount silently stops working (H-BXGY [fetch-tool]). Promised gifts get missed; G-08 has no substitute or notice.
2. **Grouping disappears in split fulfilment and returns** ("the bundle's included items display as individual line items instead", H-ELIG). The packer and the returns desk lose the bundle context exactly when they need it.
3. **Bundle price not maintained**: "When the price of one of the bundled products updates ... the bundle price doesn't update" (H-SB); and a component SKU change means "delete and recreate the entire bundle" (H-ELIG). Definitions rot.
4. **A failing transform blocks the cart** (`"cart": null`, CL-CTERR) and collisions between apps are resolved by silently discarding operations (D-CT). Both contradict G-16 "visible status, does not stall".
5. **Per-customer limit by email/phone** (H-BXGY [fetch-tool]) - trivially bypassed with a new email (G-15).

## New use cases (with source)

- **NEW-SH-1 Bundle definition becomes invalid** (component variant deleted/renamed): bundle auto-unpublished to Draft with an explicit message; operator repairs. Source: H-SB "If a bundle product repeatedly changes from Active to Draft status, then it might be because a product variant or option linked to the bundle has been removed or the title of a variant or option has been changed."
- **NEW-SH-2 Bundles and subscriptions / pre-orders are incompatible** - an order line cannot be both. Source: H-ELIG "Bundles aren't compatible with purchase options, which includes subscriptions, pre-orders, and try-before-you-buy."
- **NEW-SH-3 Bundles cannot be nested** (a bundle as a component of another bundle). Source: H-ELIG "Bundles can't contain other bundles." and D-CUST "A bundle can't have components and be part of another bundle simultaneously."
- **NEW-SH-4 Exchange of a bundle is refused** even for an identical bundle (returns-desk edge case). Source: H-ELIG "Bundles can't be used in exchanges even when exchanging for an identical bundle".
- **NEW-SH-5 Buyer-requested removal of an unfulfilled line** (incl. a bundle component or a gift) to be reviewed/declined by the merchant. Source: CL-REQEDIT "Buyers can request the removal of unfulfilled line items from an order".
- **NEW-SH-6 Ownership of a bundle definition by the app that created it** - only that app may change components. Source: D-PVRBU "After an app assigns components to a bundle, only that app can manage those components." (Relevant if OpenLinker writes bundles into Shopify: it must own them or leave them alone.)
- **NEW-SH-7 Weight and shipping of a bundle derive from components**, not from the parent. Source: H-SB "Weight calculation is derived from individual item weights, not the bundle product weight" and H-ELIG "Bundles don't have their own shipping profiles."
