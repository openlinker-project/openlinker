## Key finding up front
BaseLinker has NO gift concept in its data model. A gift is just an order line (usually added by the automatic action "Dodaj produkt do zamówienia (np. gratis)") with a price the operator types (typically 0). Everything downstream keys on "price 0" or "free item": a receipt-series option "Pomijaj produkty z ceną 0", an invoice-series option to skip free items, a buyer-page select "Wyświetl produkty z ceną 0". The single place where a channel's own gift flag is read is IdoSell (July 2026), and BaseLinker converts it into a technical 0.01 zł price with the value subtracted from the other lines. Discounts got a real model only in 2026 (April "Rabaty" tab = price-change history; June "moduł zarządzania rabatami" with order/item/shipment types, history, automatic actions and API).

## Integration normalisation (how BaseLinker deals with each channel encoding discounts/gifts differently)
- Per-integration toggles, not a central policy. Each connector gets its own setting when a customer complains. The richest is Kaufland (July 2026): import "discounts" field with type and amount, and the operator picks one of four treatments: skip, separate discount lines, merge with products, lower the product price. Yampi (March 2026): add discounts/fees into product prices or not. MerchantPro (July 2026): wallet payment becomes a named discount line ("sam nazwiesz pozycję"). BigCommerce (Feb 2023): advanced parameter split_discounts spreads a discount across lines. Generic shop file: discount as its own line or spread evenly over products.
- IdoSell gift correction (July 2026): gift-flagged lines are rewritten to 0.01 zł and their value subtracted from the other lines so totals, invoices and ERP "działają spójniej". This keeps the order total right but erases the gift as a concept and invents a price.
- Since June 2026 a central discount log (order/item/shipment, before/after, per-line breakdown) receives marketplace discounts "where the platform exposes the data", manual discounts, ERP/API discounts and discounts from automatic actions. This is the first step towards a neutral "list price + reduction" model, but documents still read only the final line price.
- Bundle normalisation on import: if a shop/marketplace line links to a catalog bundle and splitting is on, BaseLinker replaces it with components and records the price gap as a discount ("rabat"); requires product linking plus stock sync to the shop. Name stays from the channel unless "Nazwy produktów z magazynu".
- Static per-line attributes (VAT, EAN, SKU, weight) for Allegro orders are taken from the product at the moment the offer was linked; changing the product does not affect new orders until the link is re-saved. A drift risk for bundle/gift VAT.
- Rounding: multi-quantity shop lines can be split into rows with different prices because of rounding; fix is an advanced switch "og_fix".

## UX patterns worth copying
- One generic "add product to order" action that is reusable for gifts, with explicit fields Katalog / Magazyn (where stock comes from) / product lookup by ID, name, EAN, SKU or location / Nazwa / Ilość / Cena. The explicit warehouse choice matters for gift stock.
- Packer reminder as a pop-up in the packing assistant with a sound, closed by any barcode scan (no hands off the scanner).
- Discount history tab on the order card plus an API log with value_before/value_after, actor (profile_id, -1 for API) and per-line breakdown of an order-level discount. Good audit shape for X-01.
- Kaufland-style explicit choice of how imported discounts are represented, offered as a short list of named treatments rather than hidden behaviour.
- "Customer has / has no other orders" condition with a selectable look-back window, as a cheap first-order-gift guard.
- Events at the right grain: "Dodano produkt", "Usunięto produkt", "Edytowano produkt", "Zestaw został rozbity", so follow-up rules can react to gift insertion or bundle expansion.

## Anti-patterns (avoid)
- Gift = "price 0" with no flag. Every downstream feature (receipt skip, invoice skip, buyer page hide) keys on price, so a genuine 0-price item, packaging and a gift are indistinguishable, and a gift sold at 0.01 escapes all of them.
- Technical 0.01 zł price for gifts with value moved onto other lines (IdoSell correction). Distorts per-line prices, margins and VAT split; the gift's real value is gone.
- "Hide it from the document" as the gift strategy for fiscal receipts and invoices instead of list price plus documented reduction with a reason.
- Discount handling invented per connector with different names and semantics (Kaufland 4 options, Yampi boolean, BigCommerce hidden param, MerchantPro wallet line). No shared vocabulary for the operator.
- Negative-price "Rabat" line as the recommended manual way to give a discount (help). Fiscal printers and KSeF lines cannot carry it cleanly and it breaks per-line VAT attribution.

## Conflicts / open questions
- Receipt corrections: help says Base.com does not issue returns/corrections for receipts (do it on the fiscal register), but June 2026 changelog adds a separate numbering series for "paragony korygujące" in Subiekt GT by Base. Likely the correction happens in the external system only. Unresolved.
- FAQ says a return is accepted or rejected only as a whole, while Oct 2025 changelog (via search summary) mentions partial refunds for selected products. Possibly accept-whole + refund-partial. Unverified.
- "Klient ma inne zamówienia" matching key (email / Allegro login) comes from a search-engine summary, not a page I could read. Unverified.
- addOrderDiscount exists as a tester link but has no published parameters; the June 2026 module mentions "obsługa przez API". Shape unknown.
- Whether price_brutto in getOrders is pre- or post-discount when a discount log entry exists: unverified (sample shows item value_before 40 / value_after 36 while price_brutto 129.99, so the sample is not self-consistent).
- Gomag "discounts split into separate lines with voucher code" appeared only in a search summary; no URL confirmed. Unverified.
- KSeF (FA(3)) handling of 0-price gift lines and of discounts: nothing documented.

## Confidence assessment
- High: gift via "Dodaj produkt do zamówienia (np. gratis)" with listed fields; no gift flag in API; receipt/invoice "skip 0-price / free items" options; IdoSell 0.01 correction; Kaufland 4 discount treatments; June 2026 discount module and getOrders discount log fields; bundle split shows price gap as rabat.
- Low: how the bundle price gap is allocated across components; matching key of returning-customer condition; per-marketplace discount import coverage.
- Unverified hypotheses: SKU/EAN conditions with "oraz" apply to order products (blog); gift running out can be caught via "Przerwij po błędzie".
