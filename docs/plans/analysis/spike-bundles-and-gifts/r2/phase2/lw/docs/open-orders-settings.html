Open Orders: Settings

Linnworks offers Order Management Settings which help to optimize your order management workflow based on your needs. This includes error prevention checks to ensure orders cannot be processed until certain conditions are fulfilled.

Important! Keep in mind that some order settings are user-specific, meaning that each user that can log into your account needs to set them up for their own user account. Error prevention settings apply for all users but are ignored when the Batch Process method is used.

Order Management Settings are available under Settings > General Settings > Order Management. See here for full details on all available setting options.
