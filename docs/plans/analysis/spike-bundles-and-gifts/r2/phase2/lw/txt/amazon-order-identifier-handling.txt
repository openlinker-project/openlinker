 Amazon Order Identifiers Handling 
 Amazon Order Identifiers
 There are five types of order identifiers related to Amazon. These identifiers are added to orders when Linnworks downloads them from Amazon and saves them in the Open Orders screen. They can tell if the order is a premium, B2B, commercial, prime, or scheduled delivery one. When these identifiers are added to orders, it is simpler for you to spot them in Open Orders and take the necessary steps, like assigning required postal services.
 You can view these identifiers in Settings > General Settings > Order Management > Identifiers . This section automatically includes Amazon identifiers when you integrate the Amazon channel into your Linnworks account. 
 Types of Amazon Order Identifiers
 Amazon B2B Order 
 These are orders from Amazon listings with business prices. For full details on Amazon B2B selling, see this guide .
 Amazon Premium Order 
 This identifier is often applied to an order together with Amazon Prime Delivery. For full details on how to handle such orders, see this guide . 
 Amazon Prime Delivery 
 Amazon Prime is a service that Amazon sellers can offer their buyers. An Amazon Prime seller must guarantee next-day delivery on all their Prime listings. For full details on handling Amazon Prime Delivery orders (also called Amazon Next Day orders), see this guide .
 Amazon Scheduled Delivery 
 Amazon Scheduled Deliveries let buyers pick a specific day or date range for their delivery instead of dispatching the item immediately. For full details on how to handle such orders, see this guide .
 Commercial Address 
 Amazon commercial orders refer to those with a commercial delivery address. For full details on how to handle such orders, see this guide .
 Amazon FBM Ship+ Order 
Identifies orders that can be fulfilled through the Amazon FBM Ship+ program. For full details on the Amazon FBM Ship+ program, see this guide .
 Amazon Easy Ship Order  
 Identifies orders that will be fulfilled through the Amazon Easy Ship program. See this guide for more details.
 Amazon SmartConnect (SellerFlex) 
 Identifies FBA orders that will be fulfilled through the Amazon SellerFlex program (shipped from the seller’s warehouse). See this guide for more details.
 Note! The  Amazon Vendor Direct Fulfillment Order and  Amazon Vendor Retail Procurement Order identifiers are related to the  Amazon Vendor  channel integration.
