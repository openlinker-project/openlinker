 General Settings: Inventory Management 
 Inventory Management
 The Inventory management tab   contains the inventory and purchase order settings that the system will use when working with your inventory in Linnworks.net.    
 Inventory settings
 Default minimum level:  This is the minimum level that will automatically apply when a new item is created without a specific minimum level set. 
 Activate the primary button via barcode: This  will give the user an alternative option when modifying a batch by  Add batch , creating a  new bin rack  screen, and/or booking in stock, to scan this barcode (inserted in the provided field) to perform the save or book in function, providing the information is valid. 
 Scrapped Stock Consumption: By default, scrapped stock items are not included in consumption. Enable this to include scrap items in the inventory consumption statistics.   
 Purchase orders
 The default tax rate is 0:  This option controls whether tax is added to items when a Purchase Order is created. By default it is disabled. If enabled, items will be added to a PO with a VAT rate of 0. The tax can then be manually edited if necessary.  
 Additional cost types:  Click  Manage Types  to add, edit or delete cost types that are defined in purchase orders. Click  here  for more details.
